## 5. Production

### 5.1 The pipeline

- **How it works:** HTML/CSS/JS scenes on a deterministic clock. Headless Chromium captures every frame, and `ffmpeg-static` encodes it to H.264 + AAC.
- **Checks:** a copy lint plus automatic checks of colour, frame accuracy, determinism, loaded fonts and the safe box ([pipeline.md](pipeline.md) §0, §2).
- **One file per video** renders every ratio, language and theme, and reads the site's assets read-only.
- **Remotion was rejected:** its licence terms, a rewrite of the brand, and nothing it adds at this volume.
- **Proven only on a 3-second test.** Every scene component is still to build.

## 6. Music policy

Detail: [music.md](music.md) §0, §4, §5.

**Default on every channel: music written in code** (`music-lab/bed.mjs`).
- It is the studio's own work: no credit, no single-platform licence, no model involved.
- Every bed is reproducible to the byte and logged in `music-lab/ledger.csv`.
- The mood is D minor at 90 BPM: a slow pad, a chime on each plate, a tick on each text card, a held pad under prices and conditions, and one closing sting.
- One bed serves all three languages.

**Nobody has listened to it yet.** The owner's 10-minute test comes first: `music-lab/out/bed-full-s7.m4a`, `bed-full-s21.m4a`, `bed-perc-s7.m4a` and `mux-test.mp4`, played on a phone speaker and on earbuds. Until then, renders use the silent track.

**Loudness**, measured on the encoded file:
- −14 LUFS for social masters;
- −16 LUFS for long 16:9 and website files;
- true peak at −1 dBTP or lower;
- every video still works with the sound off.

**Narrow exceptions:**
- checked Pixabay tracks, only if the owner allows them (§8 Q6);
- the YouTube Audio Library, on YouTube-only uploads;
- Meta's Sound Collection or TikTok's Commercial Music Library, only for posts made and kept inside that one app.

**Never:**
- trending sounds or the general in-app libraries;
- CC BY music in short videos;
- generated music;
- the WorkspaceHQ trailer sounds (no recorded licence);
- registering the beds with Content ID.
