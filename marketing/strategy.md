## 0. The strategy on one page

1. **The goal is qualified Aperçu requests from registered businesses, not reach.** Social media is where a buyer checks the studio, not where deals close. Competitor accounts are small: Piteur has 20,730 Facebook followers, UpGrowth 196, and Pula Media 25 TikTok followers [CH §0.4, §9]. The studios that score best win through search, directories and WhatsApp [CH §0.4]. Success is counted by `?ref=` codes and conversations, never by followers [§2].
2. **One story everywhere: a site made to measure, in your language, with a price before the quote; and, from Gate B, your homepage seen before you pay.** The Aperçu is the strongest risk reversal in all three markets. No Algerian seller of the 12 scored builds anything before payment [CA §4.1]. The second pillar is native right-to-left Arabic, which is rare in Algeria, absent among global benchmarks and patchy in the Gulf [CA §4.2]. Prices are published and the site's market table cites its sources [CA §4.4]. **Until Gate B (which includes the lawyer's answer on promoting the Aperçu before the ANAE registration), the Aperçu appears only as step 3 of the method, with its conditions and no call to action** [§4, §7.1].
3. **Trust comes before any push.** The research found that what loses clients is what a buyer finds on checking: a github.io address, a personal Gmail, no registration number, no proof from others [CA §1]. Weeks 1–5 are a **soft launch**: regular posts with no invitation to write. The **Aperçu push** (its headline, its call to action, WhatsApp, the Aperçu launch trailer) starts when Gate B opens; directories and the France push also wait for Gate A [§7].
4. **One new coded master every other week, re-cuts in between.** A master is written a week ahead, reviewed in Arabic, then rendered in FR, AR and EN from one timeline and cut into Reels, TikToks, Shorts and LinkedIn 4:5 [CH §0.2; PL §5.3]. The weeks between post the AR cut, a carousel made from the master's frames and the LinkedIn PDF. That is about two posts a week per network group, at **8–12 hours a week in W1–W8** while the coded templates are built; **minimum mode posts existing cuts only** [§6.4].
5. **One account per platform, one language per post** [CH §0.1]:
   - Algeria: Facebook Page and Instagram, FR and AR.
   - France: the founder's LinkedIn first, plus the shared FR Instagram posts.
   - Gulf: TikTok and Instagram, AR and EN, kept small.
   - YouTube: the explainer library. **The website may host its MP4s once the owner decides Gate W; until then, videos live on YouTube** [§7.1].
6. **The video programme the owner asked for, at a pace one person can hold:**
   - W2: the method, « Quatre étapes » (V02), the lightest template, which builds the timeline and end card the later videos reuse;
   - W4: the **brand launch trailer** « L'atlas » (V01), a cut with no Aperçu, which is also the YouTube channel trailer;
   - W6: the **Aperçu launch trailer** (the scripted 30 s trailer), once Gate B opens; otherwise Essentiel moves up;
   - W8: the **Essentiel** explainer (V04), with a short social cut and its 16:9;
   - W10: the **L'Aperçu** explainer (V10) if Gate B is open, otherwise the **Sites & applications web** service (V11);
   - W12: V11, or the next scripted master;
   - Studio, Moteur, Identité, the other services, the 16:9 « Quelle formule pour moi ? » (V27) and the Arabic RTL piece are scripted or ready. They move into the weeks between only if the W4 review shows that a template master takes 4 hours or less; otherwise they open the next quarter.

   See §5 and §8.
7. **Everything on screen comes from the facts bank.** That means no team size, no "6 ans", no client counts or results, no testimonials, no Nomara, no stack names, no "open source", no legal claims for the studio, no payment terms, and no model names [FB §2]. Client screenshots appear only with written permission, and nobody has asked yet [FB §2.3; OC §6]. Two back doors are closed too:
   - **link previews:** every link to the site unfurls a share card that shows a Bordj Steel capture captioned « site livré », so previews are removed until that card changes (Gate 0, §7.1);
   - **own-site footage:** only named regions of the studio's site may be filmed (§3.3).
8. **Made in code, scored in code:**
   - coded motion design in the site's own engravings, themes and fonts [PL §0], under the brand's own bans: no gradient text, no blinking cursor, every loop settles (§3.3);
   - music written in code, never trending sounds or generated audio [MU §0];
   - never a face for the founder [FB §1.2].
9. **Measurement uses only what exists:** `?ref=` on every link that can carry one, a fixed code per bio, the question "Où nous avez-vous découvert ?" in the first reply, a 30-minute weekly log, and platform-native insights [CH §1.8; GP L10]. There are no pixels and no shortener services.
10. **Three reviews:**
    - W4 (28 Nov): a production and gates check.
    - W8 (by 29 Dec): compare channels and re-weight; markets are compared only after 8 weeks [GP L10].
    - W12 (23 Jan): plan the next quarter and decide whether a paid test is worth preparing. The owner chose organic only for now (2026-10-26).

### 3.3 Voice and on-screen rules (summary)

- **Tone:** calm, exact and unhurried, like the site's "engraved atlas". The brand's motion is "tuned for calm rather than spectacle" [MU §5.1].
- **Text carries the whole message.** There is no voiceover [CH §1.3]:
  - **hook:** 7 words or fewer at frame 0, held at least max(1.5 s, words ÷ 3 s), and at least 2.5 s when it has 7 words;
  - **sequential reading budget:** each block gets 0.35 s per word plus 1 s of its own reading time **before the next block enters**, and a scene lasts at least the sum of its blocks' budgets plus the transitions. Checking each block alone is not enough when blocks arrive 0.33 s apart: the trailer's S2 brings in 30 new words in 6.7 s that way [TR §4.5];
  - **the Aperçu conditions:** the full line (17 words, 6.95 s; 24 words and about 9.4 s with « 5 jours ouvrés à réception de vos contenus ») goes only in a scene that gives it that time, as the package scripts' S11 and steps block 2 do [PK §1.8, §2.1]. Elsewhere, show the short form (§3.4) and put the full line in the caption;
  - **small print:** at least 44 px on the 9:16 master, repeated in the caption;
  - **16:9:** at least 56 px for any text that must be read, and 64 px or more for the conditions; or the conditions get their own full-frame scene. On a phone held upright, a 1920 px frame is shown about 390 px wide, so 40 px renders at about 8 px [PK §1.3 and TR §3.2 set 40 px: raise it].
- **Arabic:** set 10–15% larger, line-height 1.5 or more, never letter-spaced, Western digits [CH §1.3; FB "How to use"].
- **Never mix FR and AR in one frame**, with one exception: **a product demonstration of the language switch, with French captions**, where the switch is the subject, not the copy. That covers V01 S3, V03, V05 S3, V11 S3–S4 and the Aperçu trailer's S3 [TR §4.1; PK §4.1; SV §5.2]. Posts aimed at France use the `fr-strict` render of those scenes until the lawyer's Loi Toubon answer (Gate B) [CH §3.4].
- **One safe text box on the 9:16 master, for every language: x 140–940, y 290–1240** (800 px wide). Arabic is right-aligned inside it. The button column on the right of Reels and TikTok (about 140 px wide) follows the viewer's app language, not the video's, and many Algerian and Gulf viewers run French or English interfaces, so an Arabic line may not run past x 940 [CH §1.4].
  - **Production note:** the pipeline's 9:16 safe area today lets text go down to y 1440 [PL §5.2], and the scripts mirror the Arabic box to x 140–1000 [PK §1.3] or x 140–960 [TR §3.3; SV §4.3]. In W0, encode this one box in `safeCheck()`, run it on the last frame of every scene, and check it against TikTok's LTR and RTL safe-zone files [CH S16] and on a real phone in both a French or English and an Arabic interface.
- **Own-site footage (the studio's own site).** It is allowed only as element captures of named regions, taken into the scratchpad (never with `npm run work:capture`, `shots` or `og`, which write into the repository [FB §3.2]), shown in a frame with a **blank address bar**:

## 10. Measurement without trackers

### 10.1 What exists

- **Links carry codes into every contact.** A link to the site can carry `?ref=` and `utm_*`. The form's email body gets a « Source : … » line, and every WhatsApp prefilled message ends with ` [ref]`. The ref is cleaned to `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` and cut at 20 characters (checked in `assets/app.js` at the `refTag()` function).
- **The site stores nothing** [FB §1.11].
- **Language and currency in the link:** `?lang=` and `?cur=` set the visitor's language and currency [FB §1.11].

**No pixel, no analytics script, no YouTube or Facebook embed** [CA §8.5]. Also no link-shortener or link-in-bio service: these log clicks on a third party's servers. That last point is this plan's inference from the no-tracker promise.

### 10.2 Code scheme

The scheme is `{market}-{platform}-{lang}-{id}`, at most 20 characters, `a-z 0-9 -` [CH §1.8].
- **Market** = the post's intended market in this calendar: FR and AR posts on Instagram and Facebook → `dz`; LinkedIn → `fr`; TikTok and EN posts → `gu`; YouTube → `yt`.
- The lead's real country goes into the log from the conversation.
- Codes never appear on screen; each file's code is kept in its render report (§3.4).

## 11. What we won't do

Items 1–13 are the vault's competitor analysis §8, applied to organic content. Items 14 onwards come from the standing rules and this research.

1. **Counters and statistics without a source.** This rules out project, client or year counts; satisfaction percentages; and the clients' own figures visible in screenshots, such as 25 000 t/an, 1.2K+, 98 % and 150+ [CA §8.1; FB §2.1 #7, #8].
2. **Self-awarded rankings.** No « meilleur », « n°1 », « leader », and no « le seul studio qui… ». The research found no Algerian seller that builds before payment, but it scored 12 sellers, not all of them [CA §8.2, §4.1; FB §2.1 #9].
3. **Permanent discounts or invented scarcity.** No discount. A "places left" line only with that day's real count across all channels. Take time-bound posts down after their month [CA §8.3; CM › R7].
4. **Concepts shown as client work.** The Glaive badge is overlaid in every frame. Mock-ups say « exemple ». A client site is never presented as an Aperçu. AlmaFlowClim is never shown as live on its own domain [CA §8.4; KIT §6; FB §1.9].
5. **Trackers on the site.** No pixel, no YouTube iframe, no Instagram or Facebook widget, no click-logging shortener [CA §8.5; CH §5.3].
6. **A lead database that breaks the "nothing is stored" promise.** The lead log holds the business and the stage only [CA §8.6; CM › Lead log].
7. **Lock-in language.** Maintenance is « sans engagement de durée », and that is a contrast to keep [CA §8.7; FB §1.5].
8. **Payment terms of any kind until the owner decides them,** so no "100% up front" either. Payment methods are never named in a post, a catalog item or a reply template [CA §8.8; FB §2.2].
9. **Guarantees capacity cannot keep.** No "48 h", no "réponse en 1 h", no "satisfait ou remboursé". The only promises are the site's: a first reply within 24 working hours, and the Aperçu in 5 working days once the content has arrived [CA §8.9; FB §1.7].
10. **Content in bulk.** No auto-generated posts, comments or pages. Few pages, each one true [CA §8.10].
11. **Pretending to be local.** No Gulf or French address or number presented as an office, no flags, no landmarks. "Studio based in Algeria" in every Gulf piece [CA §8.11; CM › Rules for every ad].
12. **Comparisons without a source,** and no named competitor in any post. The market table appears only with its sources [CA §8.12; CH §1.7].
13. **Letting proof decay.** A monthly link check; outdated posts come down [CA §8.13].
14. **New generated footage, images, voices or music:** no new generations [DL › Standing rules, "No more generations"]. The 25 existing generated engravings appear only as the owner allows (Gate 0), and are never described as hand-engraved, hand-made or "sans IA" [FB §3.1]. Do not reuse the one generated photographic scene from the paid-campaign creative kit [CM › The ads].
15. **A face for the founder.** Real photos only if he supplies them. `coin-king.webp` never stands in for him [FB §1.2, §2.4].
16. **Anything on the do-not-claim list:** team size; "6 ans"; « des clients en France »; stack or tool names (C1's service lines included, through the copy lint); Nomara; client counts or results; testimonials; "open source"; « pas d'intermédiaire »; « 20 % plus rapide »; studio registration or legal claims (V14's RGPD clause included, until confirmed); VAT mechanics [FB §2.1].
17. **AI model names** anywhere, including the WorkspaceHQ features screen and the trimmed service lines (c5, f3) [FB §2.1 #21].
18. **Trending sounds or general in-app music libraries.** The platform libraries are used only for posts that stay inside one app. The master carries code-made music. The WorkspaceHQ trailer sounds are not reused [MU §0, §6].
19. **Bought reach of any kind:** no engagement pods, bought followers or likes, automated invitations, messages or comments, or profile scrapers. No WhatsApp message started by the studio [KIT §5.1, §6].
20. **The "Boost" button.** Boosting a post is ad spend, and the owner's rule is organic only for now (2026-10-26).
21. **Unreviewed Arabic.** No FR and AR mixed in one frame, except a language-switch demonstration with French captions (§3.3); no English screens in posts aimed at France, so `fr-strict` until the lawyer answers [CH §2.3, §3.4; FB "Channels"].
22. **Vanity targets.** Followers, likes and views are signals, not goals (§2.4).
23. **The generated look in motion:** gradient-filled text, glow or bloom on type, loops that never settle, a blinking cursor, colours outside the site's tokens [BR › The anti-slop sweep, The logo].
24. **Client screens through a back door:** link previews that unfurl `og.jpg`; own-site footage outside the named regions (§3.3); Glaive frames without the overlaid badge.
