# Relevés — ten carousels that teach and entertain

*Plan only: nothing is generated. Built 27 Oct 2026 from research on Azuki's storytelling, on what makes informative carousels work, and on Numidea's own client map, then critiqued for entertainment, honesty and coverage.*

> **Written before the new characters.** This plan assumes no faces and no recurring guide (the founder as the monogram « Y »). Since then you chose Yasser and Kūro for the brand and the flame coder as the explainer of educational carousels. The series frame, the ten topics and the facts all still hold; the visual host changes. See "Adapting to the characters" at the end.

## The series

“Relevés” (survey notes from the atlas), a season of ten. Each carousel is one relevé: a single question a business owner faces, told through one fictional business labelled “exemple”, drawn on Numidea’s own engraved atlas, and closed by a “légende”, the recap frame worth saving. The world stays constant and only the information changes. Every cover repeats one layout: the brass grid at the top, the seal (Arcanum’s small rotated brass square) left of the kicker “NN — Relevé”, the plate watermark low right, a one-word title ending with a full stop, and a hook of seven words or fewer. Frame 2 is a second cover that restates the promise and says “Pour commencer” with the légende’s frame number. The end card is always the wordmark, the label and the pointer. Shared vocabulary: planche (a frame), relevé (an episode), légende (the recap). Each plate keeps one job (owner to approve): Pl. I Les Bibans = the path (01 Formule, 10 Porte); Pl. II the Arch = how a site is built (03, 04, 09); Pl. III the coins, horse and palm only = prices (02, 05); Pl. IV Cap Carbon = being found, with no search claim (06); Pl. V the Grand Erg = distance (the Gulf edition of 03); the lantern = night work (07); the workbench = the workshop and the founder (03, 08). One running gag, taken from the site’s own FAQ: no “bonne chance” on launch day (05, 06). English episodes say “Survey”. Numbers follow the order actually posted and are assigned when the copy locks. There is no total counter, no promised date, and no publishing day announced until the owner commits to one. A cliffhanger (“Prochain relevé”) names only the question of the next locked episode in the same market and on the same channel; otherwise the end card has none. A one-page canon sheet lists the exemple businesses (Association; Boutique de vêtements and Atelier de savon; Café; Usine, emballages; Grossiste; Jeune plateforme; École, Cabinet médical and Agence de voyages; Épicerie fine; a property agency; Société), the plates and their jobs, the codes and titles, and the recurring lines with their keys. It is checked against claims.md and the three Gate C sheets before each piece. No talking guide: the compass in 04 only draws, unless the owner chooses a faceless guide. Every episode is complete on its own. The season closes on “Porte.” once Gate B opens, and its Gate-0 fallback “Refonte.” serves the redesign buyers until then.

- A fixed caption grammar with a recurring line: hook and searchable phrase, « Relevé NN — Titre. », « Pour commencer », the pointer, one genuine question, the motto. ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E6BJ_FIjuTY))

- A small, ordinary protagonist on a quest with small stakes: each episode follows one fictional « exemple » business. The studio is never the hero and claims no outcome. ([source](https://www.zagabond.com/how-azuki-is-building-a-world-before-the-story/))
- Reward people who come back without shutting out newcomers: a « Pour commencer » line on frame 2 and in the caption, and recurring exemples kept in the canon sheet. ([source](https://www.azuki.com/news/01a0b4b5-ed41-7ddb-8961-6177d18a105b))

- Different formats, one connected world: the RTL lesson enters through a France edition and a Gulf edition, each complete on its own, with no callback across markets. ([source](https://www.azuki.com/news/019f67fc-8854-7aa4-8de9-c129b61b6f3f))

- Faceless authorship as a stance: the monogram « Y » and the workbench engraving stand in for the founder. There is no face anywhere. ([source](https://panews.io/articles/0l5nrj6v))
- A light, situational register beside the calm explainers: the phone that will not stop asking (05), the pile of sheets at 3 a.m. (07). The humour comes from the situation, never a face, with no exclamation marks and no trending sounds. ([source](https://superanimestore.com/blogs/events/azuki-streams-mini-short-3dcg-anime-by-yasuda-gensho-studio))

## Relevé 01 — Formule. Choose your path along the Bibans: which package fits? (series pilot)

- **For:** Newly registered small businesses needing a first credible presence and identity (primary). Also served: associations, cultural bodies and non-profits (fork 1 carries « Association exemple »); online sellers moving to their own shop (fork 2); startups and founders who need a business tool (fork 3).
- **Services:** Sites & applications web, Identité & systèmes visuels, Boutique & réservation
- **Where:** Algeria. Instagram carousel, where the trail reads in swipe order, plus a native Facebook Page post in which every fork tile carries its own question and destination, so the mosaic spoils nothing. French first. The Arabic edition follows the native review, with counters instead of swipe arrows and « ← » for forward. 4:5, 1080×1350, 8 frames. Prices are said once, on the map: « env. … DA » with a smaller « ≈ … € HT » line, and the conversion note in the caption. Optional Gulf edition in English (“Which package?”): the same forks, destinations by name, weeks and support only, “A price before the quote.” and “Studio based in Algeria”. Its fork 2 drops the Algeria-specific WhatsApp line.
- **The fun part:** A trail map the reader plays. One brass dashed path climbs Plate I (the three Bibans layers) and forks once per frame. Each fork asks one yes-or-no question of eight words or fewer: « oui » plants a summit flag with a package, and « non » moves the series seal on to the next fork. The reader finds their own summit. The last content frame is the whole map with every summit and its price, the recap people save. Fork 1 hides a small spot-the-difference grafted from « Enseigne. »: an association’s sign, poster and post drawn out of register, with three rings on the logo, the colour and the typeface.
- **Azuki lesson:** Give the world its places and each place a job. Azuki’s world gives every place and object a role in the story (https://www.zagabond.com/how-azuki-is-building-a-world-before-the-story/), so here the Bibans (Pl. I) carry « the path ». As the pilot, this piece fixes the frame-1 layout that every later relevé repeats, so a scroller recognises the series before reading it: Azuki’s « The art was the signal » (https://www.azuki.com/about). Frame 2 opens on a true pair in Azuki’s rhythm (‘Two elements. More possibilities.’, https://www.azuki.com/news/01a0b4b5-ed41-7ddb-8961-6177d18a105b): « Trois formules. Un prix avant le devis. »
- **Cover hook:** « Quelle formule pour moi ? »
- **What the reader learns:** Four honest questions sort a first project. No logo or charter yet: start with the identity, a documented charte (2 to 5 weeks). Selling products: an online shop with ordering by WhatsApp (5 to 10 weeks). User accounts and dashboards: Moteur, a business tool rather than a site (8 to 16 weeks, 6 months of support). Otherwise the page count decides. 1 to 5 pages is Essentiel (2 to 4 weeks, 1 month of support). 6 to 15 pages is Studio: trilingual with the Arabic built right to left, a CMS to manage the content yourself, 3 to 7 weeks and 3 months of support. Every published range is indicative; the firm price follows the scoping and does not move after it.

## Relevé 02 — Devis. Six questions before you ask for a quote, with the roles reversed

- **For:** Building trades and home-service artisans in France (primary). Also served: professional firms that quote for a living, such as architecture and engineering offices, accountants and consultants. They are named on page 2, and page 6 explains fixed-price billing.
- **Services:** Sites & applications web, Identité & systèmes visuels
- **Where:** France. The founder’s LinkedIn as an 8-page document (PDF, every page 1080×1350, flattened, titled « Relevé 02 — Devis : 6 questions avant de demander un devis »), with the link in the first comment. The shared Instagram edition ends on « Lien en bio ». French only, French-only screens. Prices are said once, as « env. … € HT », with the conversion note in the caption. Not posted on Facebook.
- **The fun part:** Role reversal on a job sheet: « Vous faites des devis toute la journée. Cette fois, c’est vous qui en demandez un. » Each page is a « bon de travaux » drawn in hairlines, on which two boxes get ticked. Each box is a question the reader answers about their own project, with the site’s own driver line under it. Page 6 turns the tables again with a one-question quiz grafted from « Forfait. » (« Une fourchette, c’est déjà un devis ? » → « Non. »). Page 7 is the whole sheet with six ticks, the page people screenshot or download.
- **Azuki lesson:** Give each episode something to keep, free and ungated. Azuki releases a keepsake art print with each chapter (https://www.azuki.com/manga); here the keepsake is the ticked job sheet, and LinkedIn lets anyone download the whole PDF. Nothing is held back and no email is collected. The page-6 turn is an honest cliffhanger in the spirit of Azuki’s own lesson on ‘transparency and expectation management’ (https://panews.io/articles/0l5nrj6v): the reveal is a rule, never a hidden price.
- **Cover hook:** « Vous faites des devis. Et le vôtre ? »
- **What the reader learns:** Six things move a website quote: scope (pages, products or screens), design (fully custom, or built from an existing charter and mock-ups), languages (each adds content; Arabic adds a right-to-left layout), integrations (user accounts, third-party tools and APIs), content (supplied or produced) and timeline (a compressed schedule costs more: +25 % in the studio’s estimator). Answered together, they make a brief. An indicative range is not a quote: the firm price follows the scoping and does not move after it. At a fixed price for a written scope, the provider absorbs any overrun.
