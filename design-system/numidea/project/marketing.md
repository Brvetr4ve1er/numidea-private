# Marketing plan

The studio's organic marketing for W1–W12 (Sun 1 Nov 2026 to Sat 23 Jan 2027), condensed from its private plan, the "Marketing & video plan — Q4 2026" doc, and the strategy, channel and facts files behind it (2026-10-26). Weeks run Sunday to Saturday, because the working week is Sunday–Thursday in Algeria and Saudi Arabia.

**What changed on 2026-10-27.** The plan was written for coded motion videos. The owner has chosen to make the marketing as designed pieces in this system instead: posts, carousels, LinkedIn documents, decks and landing pages (and short animated cuts where they help). The goal, markets, gates, rules, calendar topics and measurement below all stand. What no longer applies: the render pipeline, its build hours and the music bed. Where the plan names a video (V01, V02…), read it as the topic of that slot.

## 1. The goal

- **Qualified Aperçu requests from registered businesses, not followers: 3 by W12.** That is a planning assumption, not a forecast. Social media is where a buyer checks the studio; the sellers that score best win through search, directories and WhatsApp.
- "Qualified" means a registered business, a real website or web-app project, a budget at or above the Essentiel floor and a decision within 3 months. Only "registered" is ever said in public.
- **Targets, W1–W12:**
  - first reply within 24 working hours, every time, on every channel;
  - every Aperçu delivered within 5 working days of complete content (at most 4 a month, all sources);
  - at least 18 of the 21 planned slots published;
  - all Arabic reviewed by a native speaker before the piece is made final;
  - 0 do-not-claim items (`claims.md`);
  - a `?ref=` code on every clickable link.
- **Signals, not goals:** Instagram watch time and sends per reach, YouTube "viewed vs swiped away", LinkedIn impressions, TikTok profile visits. Follower counts, likes and views are never targets: chasing them pulls toward trends, faces and claims the studio cannot make.
- **Aperçu work always comes before marketing.** When two or more Aperçus are in progress, drop to minimum mode: no new main piece, existing pieces only.

## 5. Cadence

- **A main piece every other week.** Master week: the French piece on Tuesday, the English one on Thursday. The week between: the Arabic version on Sunday, a carousel built from the main piece on Tuesday.
- **LinkedIn:** a 4:5 post in master weeks, a PDF carousel in the weeks between, 15 minutes of comments each working day, the link in the first comment.
- **Owner time:** about 4–6 hours a week for review, posting, replies and the weekly log. Minimum mode, about 3 hours: existing pieces only.
- **Arabic review loop:** French, English and Arabic copy written from `claims.md` and approved by the owner two weeks before the post; the Arabic back from the reviewer a week before; a phone check in a Latin-script and an Arabic interface by the Sunday before. Two late reviews in a row mean a second reviewer, or a main piece every third week.
- **Swap rules:** a main piece that is not ready slips a week and its slot takes an existing piece. Never post a "lighter" version of a gated item.
- **Holidays:** Sun 1 Nov in Algeria; Wed 11 Nov in France (no LinkedIn post); the week of 25 Dec (LinkedIn optional); Fri 1 Jan; Tue 12 Jan is Yennayer (that week's carousel moves to Wed 13).
- **Reviews:** W4 (Sat 28 Nov), a production and gates check that picks path B or L; W8 (by Tue 29 Dec), compare channels and re-weight; W12 (Sat 23 Jan), plan the next quarter and whether a paid test is worth preparing.

## 7. Measurement without trackers

The site has no pixel, no analytics and no cookies, and promises none. Every link carries a code; the code rides into the email or WhatsApp message the visitor sends.

- **Format:** `{market}-{platform}-{lang}-{id}`, at most 20 characters, `a-z 0-9 -`. Markets `dz`, `fr`, `gu`, `yt`, `web`; platforms `fb`, `ig`, `tt`, `li`, `ys` (Shorts), `yl` (YouTube long-form), `wa`, `gbp`, `dir`. Examples: `dz-ig-ar-w03`, `fr-li-fr-w05`, `gu-tt-en-w04`, `yt-yl-fr-apercu`.
- **Fixed bio codes** on `https://brvetr4ve1er.github.io/Numidea.tech/`: Instagram `?lang=fr&ref=dz-ig-bio`, `?lang=ar&ref=dz-ig-bio-ar`, `?lang=fr&cur=eur&ref=fr-ig-bio`, `?lang=en&cur=usd&ref=gu-ig-bio`; Facebook `?lang=fr&ref=dz-fb-bio`; TikTok `?lang=ar&cur=usd&ref=gu-tt-bio`; LinkedIn `?lang=fr&cur=eur&ref=fr-li-bio`; YouTube `?ref=yt-bio`; WhatsApp Business `?ref=wa-bio`. `?lang=` and `?cur=` set the visitor's language and currency: France `?lang=fr&cur=eur`, Algeria `?lang=fr` or `?lang=ar`, Gulf `?lang=en&cur=usd` or `?lang=ar&cur=usd`.
- **Per post:** Facebook post text `dz-fb-{lang}-w{NN}`; Instagram Story link `dz-ig-{lang}-w{NN}`; LinkedIn first comment `fr-li-fr-w{NN}`; YouTube description `yt-yl-{lang}-{slug}`; WhatsApp Status `dz-wa-{lang}-w{NN}`. Instagram and TikTok captions carry no clickable link: the bio code counts.
- **Codes never appear on a visual.**
- **The first reply asks:** « Merci ! Pour mieux vous répondre : où nous avez-vous découvert ? » / "Thank you! One quick question: where did you find us?" / «شكراً! سؤال سريع: من أين تعرّفت علينا؟» [AR à relire], and which post they saw.
- **Lead log:** the business and the stage only, never names or numbers.
- **Weekly log, 30 minutes:** leads by code, Aperçu requests and yeses, each platform's own numbers. Compare markets only after 8 weeks.
